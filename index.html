What is cashi? +
Cashi is a fintech platform that lets you spend, send, and manage crypto stablecoins using a virtual card — anywhere in the world. Add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and pay with crypto just like a regular debit card.
How can I get the virtual cashi card? +
Join the waitlist with your email. When the app launches on the App Store and Google Play, you'll be among the first to get access to your virtual cashi card.
Where can I use cashi card for payments? +
You can use your cashi card at any physical store, online retailer, or app that accepts major card networks. It also works for contactless payments via Apple Pay and Google Pay globally.
Is cashi safe to use? +
Yes. Cashi uses industry-standard encryption and security practices. The financial products and services are issued and managed by licensed and regulated third parties. Availability is subject to local laws and applicable terms.
